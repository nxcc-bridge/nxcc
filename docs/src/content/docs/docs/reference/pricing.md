## Frequently Asked Questions

**What counts as an "invocation"?**

An invocation is counted each time one of your workers is triggered by an event. This includes every on-chain event, HTTP request, or cron tick that your worker is configured to handle.

**What happens if I exceed my plan's invocation limit?**

We believe in predictable pricing. We will notify you as you approach your limit. For Pro and Team plans, overages are billed at a transparent per-invocation rate. We will never cut off your service without warning.

**Can I self-host nXCC?**

Yes! The core nXCC node is open-source under the Apache-2.0 license. You are free to run your own nodes for development, production, or to form a federated network. Our managed IaaS platform provides a convenient, "serverless" experience with added features like premium RPC endpoints and dedicated support, but self-hosting is always an option for those who prefer to manage their own infrastructure.

**What does the Enterprise plan include?**

The Enterprise tier is a bespoke offering with custom pricing tailored to your specific needs. It can include features like:

- Dedicated, isolated infrastructure
- Custom feature development
- Enterprise-grade SLAs and dedicated support channels
- On-premise deployment options
- Security audits and compliance assistance
